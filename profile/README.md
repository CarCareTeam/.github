## Stop relying on memory

Oil changes, inspections, insurance, seasonal tires, brakes and servicing: owning a vehicle means keeping track of many different deadlines. Some depend on a date, others on mileage, and some on whichever comes first.

CarCare is a mobile-first web app that organizes all of it for you. It helps you see what is coming up, what is overdue and what work has already been completed on your vehicle.

## How it works

### 1. Add your vehicle

Choose the make, model, year and variant using the guided vehicle catalog. Complete the profile with details such as powertrain, transmission, engine size, power and current mileage.

If you have the VIN, CarCare can use it to help identify the vehicle and retrieve available technical information. You always review and confirm the details before saving them.

### 2. Choose what to track

Add tasks such as:

- engine oil and filter changes;
- brake and tire inspections;
- roadworthiness tests and insurance renewals;
- timing belt and fluid maintenance;
- seasonal vehicle care;
- cleaning and detailing;
- any custom task that matters to you.

Each task can have a date deadline, a mileage threshold or both. Tasks may be one-time or recurring.

### 3. Keep the mileage up to date

Whenever you record a new odometer reading, CarCare updates the status of distance-based tasks. Your maintenance plan follows how the vehicle is actually used—not just the calendar.

### 4. Record completed work

When a task is completed, add it to the service history with the date, mileage, cost, provider, parts and notes. You can attach a receipt and record any warranty expiry date.

For recurring work, CarCare prepares the next deadline using the interval you configured.

## A garage built around your vehicle

Vehicle selection follows a clear path:

```text
Make → Model → Year → Variant
```

Variants can include details such as fuel type, engine size and power, helping you distinguish between versions that may otherwise look identical. CarCare also preserves technical details you entered yourself: selecting a petrol catalog variant, for example, will not automatically overwrite an LPG powertrain saved by the owner.

## A dashboard focused on what matters

The main dashboard summarizes your garage and highlights:

- overdue tasks;
- tasks that are due soon;
- upcoming date-based deadlines;
- approaching mileage thresholds;
- the overall status of each vehicle;
- recent service records and documents.

Status is calculated from the information in your account. It is not a mechanical diagnosis and does not replace a professional inspection.

## Your receipts stay private

Uploaded documents are linked to your account and the relevant service record. They are not published and do not have permanent public links: access is provided through the authenticated application.

You can download documents when needed and remove them with the related data. CarCare only collects the information required to provide the service.

## Bring better information to the mechanic

**Prepare for the mechanic** brings together a concise view of:

- vehicle identity and technical details;
- current mileage;
- open and overdue tasks;
- recent service history;
- relevant receipts and warranties;
- notes you want to discuss with the workshop.

Spend less time reconstructing your vehicle’s history and more time discussing the work that needs to be done.

## Designed to stay simple

CarCare works in the browser on phones and computers. It can be installed as a web app and does not require any hardware to be connected to the vehicle.

You can explore the demo without changing real data. With a CarCare plan, you can manage your garage, create custom tasks, keep your service history and use the available advanced features.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are the suggested maintenance intervals official?

No. Initial suggestions are generic starting points. The correct intervals must always be checked against the vehicle owner’s manual or confirmed with a qualified workshop.

### Can I add a task that is not included in the suggestions?

Yes. Create a custom task with its own title, description, priority, reminders and date or mileage deadline.

### Can I manage more than one vehicle?

Yes. Each vehicle keeps its own mileage, tasks, service history and documents.

### What happens when I complete a recurring task?

The completed work is added to the service history, and the next deadline is calculated from the date or mileage recorded for that service.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. Your account and service history can be exported, and a print-friendly view is also available.

### Does CarCare replace a mechanic?

No. CarCare organizes information and reminders. It does not perform diagnostics or replace the manufacturer’s instructions, local regulations or advice from a qualified professional.
